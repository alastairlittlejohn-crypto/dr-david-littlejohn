About

Experience, expertise,
and compassionate care

Dr David Littlejohn is a specialist breast oncoplastic, endocrine and general surgeon based at the Wagga Wagga Specialist Medical Centre, and is renowned for his advanced oncoplastic techniques. He brings 26 years of subspecialist experience to every consultation.

Dr Littlejohn qualified in Sydney, completing his FRACS at Prince of Wales Hospital, Randwick. He trained in oncoplastic techniques in the UK — under Mr Rainsbury, a pioneer of the field — and returned to Australia in 2000 to establish a subspecialist practice in Wagga Wagga. He is considered a pioneering leader in oncoplastic breast surgery across Australia and New Zealand.

His centre is one of the few in the region to offer chemotherapy and radiotherapy first to shrink cancers, delivering better cosmetic outcomes for patients. He is also a co-investigator at the Riverina Cancer Care Centre for clinical trials, and his clinic has been recognised for engaging Aboriginal patients in earlier-stage breast cancer presentation.

As a founding member of Breast Surgeons NSW and BreastSurgANZ, and past Vice President of the Breast Surgical Society of Australia and New Zealand, Dr Littlejohn is deeply invested in education and training. He pioneered oncoplastic surgery cadaveric courses in Australia and NZ, convening the first Level 2 Cadaver oncoplastic workshop in Brisbane in 2014. He is a regular invited speaker at international and national conferences, and a lecturer for the Sydney University Graduate Certificate in Breast Cancer Surgery course.

Dr Littlejohn is also a specialist endocrine and laparoscopic surgeon. He is a member of the International Society of Endocrine Surgeons and has published on minimally invasive parathyroid surgery and the use of 4DCT in Wagga. He has extensive experience in breast implant-related complications, diagnosis, and treatment.

Services

How Dr Littlejohn
can help you

01

Breast & Oncoplastic Surgery

Advanced oncoplastic breast cancer surgery including flap-based reconstructions. One of the few centres offering neoadjuvant chemotherapy and radiotherapy to shrink tumours first, delivering superior cosmetic outcomes for patients.

02

Endocrine Surgery

Specialist surgical care for conditions of the thyroid, parathyroid, and adrenal glands. A/Prof Littlejohn is a member of the Australian and New Zealand Endocrine Surgeons and has particular expertise in minimally invasive parathyroid surgery, thyroid nodule management, and 4DCT-guided procedures.

03

Laparoscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive surgery using small incisions and precision instruments — offering patients significantly less pain, faster recovery, and minimal scarring compared to traditional open techniques. A/Prof Littlejohn applies laparoscopic approaches across a broad range of abdominal and endocrine procedures.

04

General Surgery

A comprehensive range of general surgical services including colonoscopy and gastroscopy for diagnosis and treatment of bowel and digestive conditions, surgical management of skin cancers and suspicious lesions — including melanoma — and hernia repair using both open and laparoscopic techniques.
